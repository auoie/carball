# The whole-system audit of 2026-11-11: what was left as it is

The audit (at 7b4d66b: 3 HIGH, 18 MEDIUM, 36 LOW) and the reviews of its fixes (RX89 to RX92) were fixed in five
commits (561298d, 6266e9a, 86b605b, f0125de and the LOWs' batch after them). The findings below were not, each because
its fix costs far more than its harm, or would undo a documented decision. One line each: what it is, and why it stays.

## Not fixed

| Finding | What | Why it stays |
|---|---|---|
| CLI-10 | A link can show a fixed-text notice (`?signin=banned&until=`) or carry a test switch (`?render=off`) into a page | Needs a marker cookie from the API and a separate e2e build (the browser tests use those switches on the shipped build). The harm is a prank: fixed strings, a black arena until a reload |
| BLD-04 | Full snapshots carry no format version | A format change on both loaders and a protocol bump, against a missed manual bump that hasn't happened; the protocol version is checked at the handshake |
| MATCH-03 (the game server's half) | A ticket issued before the process's start is refused with no clock allowance | The documented decision (AP2-4): an allowance would let a ticket spent on the last process through. The API's half is fixed (`RestartSkew`) |
| MATCH-04 (the rejoin half) | A rejoin ticket is refused while a ban from another match runs | The race it needed (a match made in the 5 s before the ban arrived) is closed: a lost leave still waiting for its ruling is ruled before any result is told (a forfeit's, the teardown's, or a win by score inside the 5 s: RV-1330), so the ban always comes before the result that frees the leaver |
| RV-1296 (the rate flag) | The network tier's new-session rate can't be set apart from the address's | No measured need; `--max-per-network` sets its sessions at once, and zero limits are now refused |
| RV-1312 (the rest) | An unconfirmed rejoin's replays each ask the API, uncharged | Needs a real account's fresh rejoin ticket and the API down; bounded by the new-session rate (5 a second an address). Charging it would refuse a real player's retry behind a busy address |
| RV-1297, RV-1315 (some tests) | No test for: the watchdog's exit in `main`, the datagram buffer's size, the worker's call of `tickTime`, the settings panel passing `before`, migration 00008's DELETE, CLI-01 as a unit | Each needs a stuck thread, a stalled link, a real worker clock or a DOM the unit tests don't have; the behaviour behind each is tested where it lives (`Watchdog`, `LocalHost.tickTime`, `mergeForSave`, the services and menu browser tests) |
| SEC-SRV-04 (the stream counts) | Four streams each way are allowed where a session uses two and three | A browser may open a spare one; the windows, which were the memory, are now 16 KB and 64 KB |
| RV-1320 (the rest) | A burst after a frame just under 100 ms, or longer than 30 frames, can still read a high rate for 10 s | Whether browsers do this needs a real device; the effect is a lag 0.8 degrees off, steady |

| RV-1331 (some tests) | No test for: the 2 KB hello cap, the windows' sizes, the once-a-minute limit on the two new log lines, the outbox's file work off the worker, a rejoin clearing the hold, CLI-13's poll | Each needs a raw QUIC peer, a captured log or a blocked disk; all were read by the reviewer |

## Noted, no change (INFO)

- RV-1299: the 3 s hello on a lossy mobile link needs a real device; a claim is now retried once when its session ends
  unanswered.
- RV-1300: an older API image on the newer schema fails a second sign-in of an identity with a row; `leaveForMatch`
  skips the 3-character name rule; at 0:00 with the scores apart and the ball in the air, the leader is settled at the
  last leave.
- RV-1305, RV-1306, RV-1322: done or within the game's own spread (notes/camera.md).
- RV-1317: a void needs a majority of the seats there (three of four can void by all dropping); it covers forfeits
  only; `openSeat` retries any failure that isn't a refusal; the watchdog's exit only helps where the restart policy
  restarts on exit 1; REN-04 and REN-05 need a real device.
- The game server's NET-07 fix (a refill ends at the queue's 60 frames) is ours, not the game's: the game's numbers
  are kept, only the state it couldn't leave is.
- RV-1335: the video guard's probe also runs on a 60 Hz page at about 40 fps (one and two refreshes a frame): 1.3 s
  at half resolution, once a browser session, and no step (a 60 Hz screen's frames can't get faster). Left: the same
  reading on a 120 Hz screen is the GPU-bound case the probe is for.
- RV-1337: no test pins the air view's jump factor short of it firing always (3 down to 1.2 fails nothing: it never
  fires in normal flight either way, by the reviewer's count), nor `main.ts`'s telling the meter every frame (the
  meter's side is tested). With the two-measures rule gone, 165 Hz with 2 ms of jitter reads 180 or 200: a constant
  0.05 degrees of lag.
