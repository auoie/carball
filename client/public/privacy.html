Privacy

Carball's offline modes (Free Play, Custom Training, Play vs Bots) need no account and send nothing about you anywhere. Your settings and training packs stay in this browser's storage.

Online play needs an account. This page says what an account stores and how to delete it.

What an account stores

  • The account number of the sign-in you used: Google's or Steam's id for you. Never your e-mail address or your name from Google; we never ask Google for them. A Steam sign-up reads your public Steam name once, only to suggest a display name; it isn't stored.
  • The display name you choose, which other players see.
  • Your ratings, for as long as the account exists, and the results of your ranked matches (who played on which team, who won, who left, how long each played, the ratings before and after), each kept for one year.
  • A queue ban after leaving ranked matches, with its end: each step forgiven after a day without leaving.
  • Your sessions: a cookie that keeps you signed in, for up to 90 days, and a short-lived cookie while a sign-in or sign-up is under way.
  • A ban, if one is ever given, with its reason and end.

What it doesn't store

  • E-mail addresses, or the access tokens Google and Steam hand us at sign-in (used once, then dropped).
  • IP addresses, in the account's records. The service holds them in memory to limit how many sign-ins and sign-ups one network can make, and the game server to limit how many connections one network can open.

Logs

The services log IP addresses, account ids and display names, with what each connection did (signing in, searching, joining and leaving a match, and anything refused), for security, abuse prevention and debugging. The logs are kept until the servers' log rotation overwrites them (about 100 MB for each service) and are never shared. They never hold your cookies, sign-in codes or tokens.

Others involved

  • Google or Steam, whichever you sign in with, under their own privacy policies.
  • Cloudflare: its Turnstile check runs when you make an account, and its network carries the site's traffic, under Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Deleting your account

Account → Delete Account removes the account at once: its sign-ins, sessions and ratings. Ranked results you played in stay for the other players, without your account in them. A keyed hash of the sign-in's id is kept for 30 days, so that the same sign-in can't make a new account in that time, then removed; a ban stays with the sign-in it was given to.
